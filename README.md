Trials Of The Primitive
Overview

Trials Of The Primitive is a first/third-person action, survival and crafting game developed in Unity for ECS657U Multi-platform Game Development.

The player has been transported into a hostile unfamiliar dimension and must survive 5 days for the portal home to reopen. 
During the day, the player explores procedurally generated grass and desert biomes, gathers resources, crafts equipment and prepares for the night. 
At night, hostile enemy waves spawn around the player and become progressively stronger as more days are survived.

The player wins by surviving the 5 days, after the night on the 5th day the player has won. The player loses if either their health or hunger reaches zero.

How to Play
Load Menu Scene to begin.


The general gameplay loop is:

Explore the procedurally generated world during the day.
Collect resources from the grass and desert biomes. (begin with wood)
Craft your first wood pickaxe.
Gather further materials using your pickaxes.
Check the recipe page to look for what you're aiming to craft next.
Open the inventory and use the 3x3 crafting grid to craft weapons, equipment, consumables and other useful items.
Equip armour and accessories to improve player stats to improve survival chances.
Manage health and hunger using collected or crafted consumables.
Prepare weapons and a shield before night begins.
Survive the hostile enemy waves that spawn during the night.
Repeat the cycle as enemies become stronger and more numerous.
Survive the required number of days to win.

Controls can be viewed and rebound through Settings → Controls. The game supports movement, sprinting, jumping, attacking, blocking, inventory interaction, first/third-person camera switching and enemy lock-on.
For enemy lock on, if you move your mouse to the left or right, you change the target of the camera lock.

The Escape menu pauses the game and provides access to settings, the recipe page and other menu options.


Resource Gathering

You can only destroy a sandstone block with a wood pickaxe and stronger. You can only destroy iron block with sandstone pickaxe. And you can only destroy diamond block with an iron pickaxe.
You should initially aim to get wooden gear to help you survive your first nights.
If you kill cactus and get cactus sap, and destroying trees or sunflower enemies has a chance to drop plant sap. You can use these to craft health potions, which are very useful for surviving.
Foxes are initially slower than you and are an easy source of meat when you can't challenge anything else.


Combat

The game contains melee combat with several approaches to fighting.

Weapons include different characteristics such as attack speed, damage and effective attack area.

Examples include:

Daggers, which allow faster and more agile combat.
Longswords, which attack more slowly but deal greater damage and have a larger effective area.
Shields, which allow the player to actively block and reduce incoming damage.
Unarmed attacks when no item is currently held.

Attack animations use collision windows so enemies are only damaged during the appropriate part of an attack.


Equipment

The player can equip armour and accessories to different attachment points.
Press right click on an item in your inventory to wear it, Or left click while it is in your main hand. 
Attempting to equip something twice, or something of the same set (2 pendants) will only replace the equipped variant with the new one.

Equipment can modify statistics including:

Maximum health
Movement speed
Attack speed
Damage
